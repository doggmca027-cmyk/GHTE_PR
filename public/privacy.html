This policy explains what personal data the GRAM Hub Telegram Mini App and its bot (the "Service") collect, why, who receives it and what choices you have. It applies together with our Terms of Service. "We" means the operator of the bot.

1. Data we collect

We do not ask for your phone number, email, real name, passwords, or your wallet's private keys or seed phrase. We never receive them: wallet connection uses the TON Connect standard and you approve each payment in your own wallet.

2. What we do with it

  • Provide the Service: sign you in, process deposits, place and track orders, issue refunds, run the referral programme.
  • Send you service messages in the bot: deposit received, order completed, cancelled or partially completed. These are transactional, not marketing.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms (including suspending accounts) and comply with legal obligations.
  • Support: investigate payment or order problems you report.
  • Improve the app using aggregated usage events.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Who receives data

  • Promotion providers that fulfil your orders receive only the link you want promoted and the quantity. They do not receive your Telegram identity from us. Links you provide are by nature public content or profiles.
  • Telegram delivers the Mini App and bot messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Infrastructure providers that host the app and database (Supabase and Vercel) process data on our behalf.
  • The TON network and a blockchain data API: deposits are public blockchain transactions; we query a public API to confirm them. Blockchain data is public and permanent by design.
  • An exchange-rate service: we fetch a public TON/USD price; no personal data is sent.
  • Authorities or advisers, where required by law or to protect our rights.

4. What is stored on your device

The app does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It keeps a few settings in your device's local storage: your chosen language and theme, and a marker that an invite link was already applied. You can clear them by clearing the app's data in Telegram.

5. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Financial records (balances, deposits, orders, refunds, referral earnings) are kept as long as needed for accounting, dispute handling and legal obligations, even after you stop using the Service, because the ledger must stay complete and unaltered. Usage events and technical logs are kept for a limited period.

6. Security

Access to data is restricted by role-based database rules; money movements are recorded in an append-only ledger; secrets are stored only on our servers; traffic is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ask us what data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We act on requests where the law allows. Some records (for example financial transactions) must be retained and cannot be deleted.
  • Stop receiving bot messages at any time by stopping or blocking the bot. You will then not be told about deposits and orders.
  • Disconnect your wallet at any time from the Wallet screen.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or Ukraine), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a minor has used the Service, contact us and we will remove the account where possible.

9. International transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process data in countries other than your own. We choose providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the current version. If a change is significant we will tell you in the bot.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, write to the bot __BOT_NAME__ or open a request in the app's Support section.
