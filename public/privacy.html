Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data this Telegram Mini App and its bot (the "Service") collect, why, who receives it and what choices you have. It applies together with our Terms of Service. "We" means the operator of the bot.

1. Data we collect

DataSourceWhy
Telegram user ID, username, first name, language codeTelegram, when you open the Mini App (verified cryptographically)To create and secure your account, show your name, and send messages in your language
Wallet address you pay from, deposit amounts, transaction hashesThe TON blockchain, when you make a depositTo confirm and credit deposits, prevent double crediting, and keep financial records
Balance and transaction history (deposits, purchases, refunds)Generated by the ServiceTo operate your prepaid balance and show your history
Orders: the link you provide, quantity, price, statusYouTo fulfil the order, show its progress and handle refunds and support
Technical data: IP address, device and request logsOur hosting and infrastructure providersTo run, secure and troubleshoot the Service, and to detect abuse

We do not ask for your phone number, email, real name, passwords, or your wallet's private keys or seed phrase. We never receive them: wallet connection uses the TON Connect standard and you approve each payment in your own wallet.

2. What we do with it

  • Provide the Service: sign you in, process deposits, place and track orders, issue refunds.
  • Send you service messages in the Telegram bot: deposit received, order completed, cancelled or partially completed. These are transactional, not marketing.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms and comply with legal obligations.
  • Support: investigate payment or order problems you report.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Who receives data

  • SMM providers that fulfil your orders receive only the link you want promoted and the quantity. They do not receive your Telegram identity from us. Links you provide are by nature public content or profiles.
  • Telegram delivers the Mini App and bot messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Infrastructure providers that host the app and database (for example Supabase and Vercel) process data on our behalf.
  • TON network and a blockchain data API: deposits are public blockchain transactions; we query a public API to confirm them. Blockchain data is public and permanent by design.
  • Exchange-rate service: we fetch a public TON/USD price; no personal data is sent.
  • Authorities or advisers, where required by law or to protect our rights.

4. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Financial records (balances, deposits, orders, refunds) are kept as long as needed for accounting, dispute handling and legal obligations, even after you stop using the Service, because the ledger must stay complete and unaltered. Technical logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

5. Security

Access to data is restricted by role-based database rules; money movements are recorded in an append-only ledger; secrets are stored only on our servers; traffic is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Ask us what data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We will act on requests where the law allows. Some records (for example financial transactions) must be retained and cannot be deleted.
  • You can stop receiving bot messages at any time by blocking or stopping the bot. Service messages are not required to use the app, but you will then not be notified of deposits and orders.
  • You can disconnect your wallet at any time from the Wallet screen.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a minor has used the Service, contact us and we will remove the account where possible.

8. International transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process data in countries other than your own. We choose providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the current version. If a change is significant we will tell you in the bot.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, write to us in the bot's chat or use the contact shown on the bot's profile.
